🎸 Guitar Practice Companion

Pick a key and work through the theory, scales, and shapes that go with it.
1. Notes in the Key
The seven diatonic chords built on each scale degree, with quality and scale-degree number.
2. Open Position Chords
Best available low-fret voicing for each chord in the key. Not every key has a true "open" (nut) shape — some show a low-position voicing instead.
3. Scales & Arpeggios

Major Scale

Arpeggios (each diatonic chord)

4. Barre Chords
Each chord shown as a movable E-shape (root on the low E string) and A-shape (root on the A string) barre chord.
5. CAGED System
Pick a chord from the key, then see all 5 CAGED shapes (C, A, G, E, D) moved to that chord's root.
6. Random Chord Progression
Generates 4 chords drawn from the key so you can practice changing between them.
